Counter-UAS interceptor

Lead engineer at SkyIntellect Inc, Mississauga, 2025

I ran the full development of a small tactical interceptor for Canadian defence missions, from first concept to working prototype, built to compete in Canada's Counter-UAS Sandbox.

Drone threats change almost daily, so the design put low cost, easy field repair and fast production first: weeks, not months.

  • Split the airframe into four sections, each sized to print in one piece on a 256 mm build plate: PETG nose with the targeting camera, PETG forward fuselage, CF-nylon aft fuselage carrying the wings and motors, and a PETG tail.
  • Stacked the avionics in a vertical column inside the cone, with the wiring order planned so every connection stayed reachable as each layer went in.
  • Used heat-set threaded inserts for bolted joints, so a damaged section can be reprinted and swapped without rebuilding the airframe.

780 kg MALE UAV airframe

Project engineer at Bharat Electronics Limited, with ADE (DRDO), 2021–24

An indigenous medium-altitude, long-endurance aircraft for intelligence, surveillance, target acquisition and reconnaissance missions. I designed the airframe and led its structural layout, assembly design and released documentation.

  • Designed a CFRP sandwich main spar for the canard configuration: 15% better structural efficiency and 10% lighter than the baseline, taken through to production drawings and tooling.
  • Built the fuel tanks into the composite wing strake, with a colour-coded 3D sealing model that guided every assembly step. The first leak test passed with zero leaks and no rework.
  • Designed system installations with the stress, aerodynamics, propulsion, avionics, electrical and landing gear teams, resolving every interface with the structure.

15 kg loitering munition

Development lead at Bharat Electronics Limited, 2021–24

A man-portable, single-use precision strike system that stays under operator control until impact. I led full-cycle development of the airframe and its integration with six subsystems: EO/IR, autonomy, propulsion, ground control, launcher and payload.

  • Folding wings let the airframe stow inside a tube launcher that breaks down into loads a small crew can carry and set up without special tools.
  • Defined the payload bay, mounting and structural interfaces, keeping centre of gravity and structural margins within limits so the payload fitted without changing the airframe.
  • Flew a wooden prototype with carbon sheeting for autonomy testing, with an all-composite delta design planned for higher speed and lower signature.

Amphibious water-sampling UAV

Project assistant at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, 2019–21

A UAV that lands on a flowing river, draws a water sample and flies home. This is the aircraft on the drawing at the top of the page.

  • Sized the stepped planing hull from seaplane practice: a flared bottom to divert spray, a step to break water drag, and a tapered afterbody for high-incidence take-off.
  • Put each material where it works best: foam with composite lay-up for every surface that meets the water, and a light wooden primary structure for the wing and fuselage that can be repaired in the field.
  • Assessed the E421 high wing in XFLR and checked it with ANSYS CFD pressure and streamline studies.

VTOL surveillance UAV

Project assistant at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, 2019–21

A hybrid quadplane that takes off and lands vertically with no runway, then transitions to efficient fixed-wing cruise for long surveillance coverage. I took it from concept to flight.

  • Designed a 2.5 m tapered high wing in rib-and-spar construction, with ribs threaded onto tubular spars to hold the Rhode 36 aerofoil.
  • Two tubular booms carry the four lift motors and join at the rear to support an inverted V-tail. The fuselage is birch ribs under a monocoque skin.
  • Kept the airframe to 3.35 kg of an 8 kg maximum take-off weight, checked in static FEA with a factor of safety of about 1.5.
